## 2. Structure

| Layer | Count | Who they are | What they own |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Centres** | 18 | Existing | Roll-up, support, accountability |
| **Pin-charges** | ~135 | Top referrers + centre volunteers | One pincode (big ones get 2–3). Not team leaders — *team makers*. Their job is "how do I get more teams in my area" |
| **Captains** | 10,000 | Anyone. Meditator or not | Build a team, collect minutes, spawn other captains |
| **Team members** | ~80,000 | Family, colleagues, neighbours, classmates | Meditate with their team |

Bangalore has ~110 pincodes (560001–560300, plus peripheral 561/562). Weight the allocation — 560066 Whitefield and 560103 Bellandur need 2–3 pin-charges; small peripheral codes can be clustered.

**Teams are not geographically bound.** Someone in Basavanagudi can run a team at their college in Rajajinagar. The pincode is where the *pin-charge* works, not where the team lives. Say this explicitly or people will over-constrain themselves.

**Beyond the 18 centres:** the coordinators' exercise already surfaced ~65 potential sub-centre / micro-centre locations plus the 15 prominent locations. Use those as the second-tier geographic anchors — they are already mapped.

## 3. Why teams change the arithmetic

The reason 10,000 volunteers looked like it produced too few minutes is that we were modelling a volunteer as someone who runs **one event**. A team is **persistent** — it meditates repeatedly through December.

| Teams | Avg size | Sessions in Dec | Minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6,000 | 6 | 6 | 1.5M |
| 8,000 | 8 | 8 | 3.6M |
| **10,000** | **8** | **8** | **4.5M** |
| 10,000 | 10 | 12 | 8.4M |

Revised December bridge:

| Lever | Minutes |
|---|---|
| Existing Bangalore organic base | 4.00M |
| Uplift on existing base (+35%) | 1.40M |
| **Teams** (10,000 × 8 × 7 min × 8 sessions) | **4.48M** |
| **Schools direct** (300 × 600 students × 50% × 7 min × 5 days) | **3.15M** |
| Corporates + institutions | 0.63M |
| SSB / temple December footfall | 0.84M |

| **TOTAL** | **14.90M** |
| **Incremental over baseline** | **10.90M** |

With the team construct and government school access, **10M incremental becomes reachable** — which it wasn't yesterday. That is worth saying tonight, because it changes what can honestly be promised in the note.

*Caveat: this rests on government school permission (not secured) and Prasanna's 40% ambassador rate (a field impression, not measured data). Present both as assumptions, not facts.*

## 4. Schools are a recruitment engine, not just a minutes engine

This got treated as an outreach line item this morning. It is bigger than that.

| Schools | Students reached | Ambassadors @40% | Team captains @10% of ambassadors |
|---|---|---|---|
| 150 | 90,000 | 36,000 | 3,600 |
| **300** | **180,000** | **72,000** | **7,200** |
| 500 | 300,000 | 120,000 | 12,000 |

A student who takes it to their parents and friends *is* a captain under the new construct — they have a team. At 300 schools, the school channel alone could produce most of the 10,000 captains, for roughly 600 volunteer-visits.

Nothing else in the plan has that leverage ratio. **Recruiting adult captains one at a time is the expensive path; schools are the cheap one.** Resource accordingly: centre-wise school POCs plus 3 supporting volunteers per centre, as proposed this morning, is the right shape — but it should be the *largest* team in the mobilisation effort, not a side track.

Two things to protect: the daily-follow-up finding (the school visited daily massively outperformed the one visited once), and certificates for everyone rather than top-three-only.

## 6. Gamification spec

Two currencies, deliberately separate, as agreed this morning.

**Currency A — Minutes.** Earned by the team collectively, never individually. Weighted so that breadth beats depth: minutes from a *new* team member are worth more than the tenth session from an existing one; a session run in an institution (school, office, college) is worth more than a solo sit. This is how you defeat the 80/20 skew — make the currency itself reward spreading.

**Currency B — Teams spawned.** Earned by inspiring someone else to become a captain. This is the viral loop and it must be visibly rewarded on its own, not folded into minutes.

**Badges** (illustrative): first team formed · 5-day streak · 11-day streak · mentored 3 new captains · team of 25 · first institutional session · pincode fully covered.

**Leaderboards:** team, pincode, centre. Publish per-capita alongside absolute so a small pincode can win. Weekly resets so late entrants can still top a board.

**The glow.** Santosh's idea deserves to survive the edit: when a team is meditating, it lights up — on a live city map, and as a shareable card. The bigger the team and the more of it meditating together, the bigger and brighter the circle. Watching Bangalore light up through December is the campaign's single best organic content asset, and it costs nothing per share.

## 7. Incentives

Three principles, all of which came out of this morning:

**Team-based, never individual.** An individual reward makes the top 20% optimise their own number and stop recruiting. A team-based reward forces spreading, because the only way to grow the team's currency is to bring more people in.

**Connected to the cause.** The strongest point made this morning: people need to feel they are doing something for the wellbeing of others, not earning a ticket. So frame the reward as *what your team unlocked for Bangalore*, not *what you won*.

**Threshold, not rank.** If the Sadhguru event access goes to the top three teams, everyone else disengages in week two. If it goes to every team that clears a bar, all 10,000 push. Leaderboards for glory, thresholds for rewards.

What to offer, in order of pull:
- **Access to Sadhguru's exclusive Bangalore events** — allocated to teams that clear a threshold, distributed by the captain to their members. The captain giving out passes is itself a recruiting act.
- **Passes and program scholarships the captain distributes** — makes the captain a person of consequence locally
- **Reserved Makar Sankranti seating** for teams that hit their December target
- **Certificates for every participant**, physical for the top tier — not top-three-only
- **Visible identity** — a band or badge that makes captains findable on the ground and makes bystanders want one

Explicitly out: cash, vouchers, merchandise prizes, and the world record.
