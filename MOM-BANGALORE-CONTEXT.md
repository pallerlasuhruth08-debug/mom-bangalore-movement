## 1. The campaign in one paragraph

Sadhguru announces that Bangalore will meditate for **10 million minutes in December 2026** (counting window 1–31 Dec). The metric is deliberately **minutes, not app downloads**, so celebrities, partners and the public can rally around it without it reading as a product push. Delivery runs on an **on-ground volunteer movement** — target ~10,000 active volunteers built between August and November — plus Sadhguru's five Bangalore celebrity/college events in late November. The whole thing feeds **Makar Sankranti, 15 January 2027**.

## 3. The operating construct (settled 31 July)

**The unit is a team, not a person.**

- **Anyone can become a captain at any time.** Meditator or not — Sadhguru has already permitted potential meditators to volunteer.
- A captain **builds a team** (family, colleagues, neighbours, classmates — anyone, anywhere in the city) and the team **collects minutes together**.
- A captain also **spawns other captains.** This is the viral loop.
- **Two separate reward currencies:** (A) minutes collected by the team, (B) teams spawned. Badges and points on both. Leaderboards at team / pincode / centre level.
- Teams are **not geographically bound.** Someone living in Basavanagudi can run a team at their college in Rajajinagar. The pincode is where the *pin-charge* works, not where the team lives.

**Santosh's "glow" idea** — teams light up on a live city map while meditating, bigger and brighter with more members, shareable — is the campaign's best organic content asset. Keep it.

## 5. The data position

| Fact | Value | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Bengaluru MOM user base | **293,569 registered** | Ishangam, 31 Jul |
| Of those, resolved to a Bangalore pincode | 71,750 (24% sample) | Ishangam |
| Active meditators, Bangalore | ~35,978 | Ishangam, 10 Jul |
| Users with a phone number on record | 170,776 of 293,569 | Ishangam |
| **Sessions / minutes / last-active** | **NOT AVAILABLE** | Ishangam holds none |

**Ranking is trustworthy; absolute counts are not.** Which pincodes are dense is based on 71,750 real observations. The per-pincode user estimates distribute the full 293,569 using the matched sample's shape, which assumes matched users are geographically representative — they are people Isha already holds a contact record for, so that assumption is doing real work. State it whenever the number is quoted.

### 🔴 The biggest open risk

The **4M minutes/month organic baseline** — which the entire December bridge rests on — was derived from "~300–350K *monthly* users × ~12 min average session." But Ishangam's 293,569 is **cumulative registrations, not MAU**, and holds no activity data at all. If Bangalore's true MAU is materially lower, the organic baseline is overstated and **10M becomes harder, not easier**. This must be settled before the number is announced. It is the central question in `mom-app-data-request.md`.

Related: volunteer recruitment realistically draws on **engaged** users (≥4 sessions/30d), not merely *active* ones. Someone who meditated once last month will not host a sit for ten people.

### The recruiting-list insight

Santosha shows **active referrers** per pincode — people who have already, unprompted and unincentivised, got others onto the app. That is a pre-qualified pin-charge. Rank each pincode's referrers, take the top 3–5, and Stage 1 becomes *"call the people already doing this for free"* rather than *"find 100 people."* Highest-leverage single move available.

**Gross vs incremental must be stated explicitly in the note.** Recommendation: announce ~10M gross publicly, track incremental internally. Presenting one number publicly and another internally is normal; conflating them is what causes trouble in January.

### Volunteer target arithmetic — it does not yet close

3% of the app estimate sums to **8,806**, not 10,000. Worse: only 170,776 users have a phone on record; 3% of that contactable subset is **5,123**. Add the centre ecosystem's realistic ceiling of 2,736 → **7,859**. **In-app notification, reaching the ~123,000 users with no phone on record, is what closes the gap** — which makes the 1 Nov in-app date a critical-path dependency, not a nice-to-have.

The **3% response assumption is itself unvalidated.** If the true rate is 1.5%, every target halves. Pressure-test on Tier 1 in August while there is still time to change course.

## 7. Schools — the highest-leverage channel

Prasanna, 31 Jul: 2–3 permissions per 10 private schools today, but **government clearance is close**, which would open **300–500 schools**. And ~40% of students in a session become active ambassadors who carry it to parents and friends.

| Schools | Students | Ambassadors @40% | Team captains @10% |
|---|---|---|---|
| 150 | 90,000 | 36,000 | 3,600 |
| **300** | **180,000** | **72,000** | **7,200** |
| 500 | 300,000 | 120,000 | 12,000 |

Under the new construct, a student who takes it to parents and friends **is a captain** — they have a team. At 300 schools this channel alone could produce most of the 10,000, from roughly 600 volunteer-visits. **Recruiting adult captains one at a time is the expensive path; schools are the cheap one.** Staff accordingly — centre-wise school POC + 3 supporting volunteers per centre.

Two findings to protect: **daily follow-up is the whole game** (the school visited daily massively outperformed the one visited once), and **certificates for everyone**, physical for the top tier — not top-three-only.

*Caveats: government permission not secured; the 40% is a field impression, not measurement.*

## 8. Incentives

**Three principles:** team-based never individual (individual rewards make the top 20% optimise their own number and stop recruiting) · connected to the cause (people need to feel they're doing something for others' wellbeing, not earning a ticket) · **threshold not rank** (if the reward goes to the top three, everyone else disengages by week two).

**Design test:** *can a bystander see it, and can they get it?* Visible + attainable recruits. Invisible or unattainable doesn't. The strongest incentive is therefore **something the captain gives away**, not receives.

**In:** access to Sadhguru's exclusive Bangalore events (allocated to teams clearing a threshold, distributed by the captain) · passes and program scholarships the captain distributes · waitlist nominations · prasad to hand out at every sit · reserved Makar Sankranti seating · certificates for everyone · visible identity band/badge.

**Out:** cash, vouchers, merchandise prizes, top-three-only competitions.

### ❌ The world record is dead
Raised in every movement; Sadhguru has consistently rejected it. **Remove from all decks.** If a record happens incidentally, fine — it is not a goal and not a motivator. *(This corrects earlier drafts of the phase plan.)*

## 9. Retention — where grassroots campaigns actually die

Yoga Veera: **1,000 registered → 350 trained → 60 who conducted a session.** The catastrophic loss is between *trained* and *did it once*, not at signup.

Three fixes: **first sit within 7 days of signing up** with the cell leader present · **human call within 48 hours** (not a WhatsApp broadcast) · **visible attribution from day one** (Bhikshu's enrolment measurably dropped when micro-pages weren't ready).

**Assume 40% attrition.** To have 10,000 active on 1 December, sign up ~14,000.

Historical base rates for sanity: Bhikshu ~1,000 signed up → 600 active, 128 crossed ₹9L · Inner Engineering Wave ~300–400 on ground at peak, ~2,000–2,500 involved across all functions over months · Rally for Rivers took 3–4 months from guidance to activation.
