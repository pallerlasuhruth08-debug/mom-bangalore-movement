# MOM Bangalore On-Ground Movement — Phase-Wise Plan

**Drafted:** 31 July 2026 · **Campaign month:** 1–31 December 2026 · **Runway to Makar Sankranti:** 15 January 2027

**Scope assumption:** this covers the *whole* movement across all six verticals, with the volunteer track (Suhruth + Santosh) detailed deepest since that is the binding constraint. Adjust if leadership wants a narrower cut.

---

## 0. The two structural ideas this plan rests on

Everything below follows from two claims. If you disagree with either, the phasing changes.

**Idea 1 — Don't scale one tactic 17×. Add one layer of branching.**
The meeting's despair about 10,000 volunteers came from comparing it to 600 (Bhikshu) or 60 (Yoga Veera) and seeing a 17–160× jump. But those campaigns were *flat*: a central team recruited every volunteer directly. A cascade doesn't need any single layer to do something unprecedented:

| Layer | Count | Ask of each person | Precedent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Centre anchors | **54** (3 per centre × 18 centres) | Own their centre's target, recruit 10 captains | Trivially done before |
| Captains | **540** | Recruit and hold ~18 volunteers | ≈ Bhikshu's 600 active — a number you have hit |
| Volunteers | **~9,700** | 2 sessions of ~10 people in December | New, but the ask is tiny |

Each layer only has to do something you have already proven at that scale. The 10,000 is an *output* of the structure, not a target anyone has to heroically hit.

**Idea 2 — Schools are your most proven lever and got almost no airtime.**
The single hardest data point in the meeting: ~4,000 downloads in June from **three or four schools** running the week-long mental-awareness challenge. Smrithi's finding that daily follow-up is the whole game (school 1, no daily visits → poor response; school 2, daily visits → many more sign-ups) makes this a *repeatable operating procedure*, not luck. Bangalore has thousands of schools. Scaling 15 → 150 is the highest-confidence million-minutes lever on the board and it needs two volunteers per school — which is exactly what the cascade produces.

## 1. Where 10 million minutes actually comes from

No one drew this bridge in the meeting. Here is a defensible version. Every assumption is stated so it can be argued with.

| # | Lever | Assumption | Minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Existing Bangalore base (organic)** | ~300–350K monthly users × ~12 min avg session | **4.00M** |
| 2 | **Uplift on existing base** | +35% from streaks, leaderboards, city pride | **1.40M** |
| 3 | **Schools** | 150 schools × 600 students × 60% participation × 7 min × 5 days | **1.89M** |
| 4 | **Colleges** | 40 colleges × 1,500 students × 40% × 7 min × 3 days | **0.50M** |
| 5 | **Corporates** | 150 companies × 300 staff × 50% × 7 min × 4 sits | **0.63M** |
| 6 | **Volunteer-led community sits** | 10,000 volunteers × 2 sits × 10 people × 7 min | **1.40M** |
| 7 | **SSB + temple December footfall** | 60,000 unique visitors × 7 min × 2 sits | **0.84M** |
| 8 | **Sadhguru events + celebrity chain + digital pledges** | 5 events, live + online pledges | **0.40M** |
| | **TOTAL** | | **11.06M** |
| | **Incremental over baseline** | | **7.06M** |

**Read this carefully before Saturday.** The bridge clears 10M *gross* with ~10% slack. It does **not** clear 10M *incremental* — that would need roughly a doubling of schools and corporates, or a second December peak week. So the note to Sadhguru must be explicit about which number is being promised. My recommendation: **announce 10M gross as the city record** (it is a real, externally verifiable city achievement and 4M of it is genuine ongoing meditation, not padding), and **track ~7M incremental internally** as the campaign's actual performance measure. Presenting one number publicly and a different one internally is normal and honest — conflating them is what gets you in trouble in January.

Note also that levers 3, 4, 5 and 6 all consume the *same* volunteer pool. The 10,000 is not doing community sits *and* running schools — the cascade has to allocate. Budget roughly: 300 volunteers on schools, 150 on colleges, 200 on corporates, the remainder on community and digital.

## PHASE 0 · LOCK — 31 July to 17 August

**Goal:** convert a good meeting into decisions, and do not lose the consecration.

### The one thing that cannot wait
The consecration volunteers are arriving **now** — ~2,000 people already receiving e-passes, some travelling tomorrow, staying 7–11 days. This is the largest concentration of motivated Isha volunteers in Bangalore that will exist before December, and it is happening **before** the note to Sadhguru is even approved.

Do not wait for approval to harvest it. You do not need a campaign name to ask someone "would you be part of something big for Bangalore in December — can we call you?" Run a simple intent capture across the full consecration cohort: a QR code, a one-line ask at the end of each day's seva, a form with name / area / what they can offer. Target **1,000+ expressions of interest** by 17 August. Those become your candidate pool for the 540 captains, and Phase 1 starts with a warm list instead of a cold one.

*[This is the single highest-leverage action in the entire plan and it expires in about two weeks.]*

### Decisions to close
| Decision | Owner | By |
|---|---|---|
| Headline number: 10M gross vs. incremental — state which | Leadership | 1 Aug (in the note) |
| Campaign name + cause line | E-media + ground insight | 10 Aug |
| Platform spec signed off (see below) | Product + Santosh | 12 Aug |
| Volunteer allocation across levers (schools / colleges / corporates / community) | Suhruth + Santosh | 12 Aug |
| Whether Makar Sankranti is in this note or handled separately | Leadership | 1 Aug |

### Platform spec — the make-or-break dependency
Bhikshu's enrolment visibly dropped when micro-pages weren't ready at peak. Do not repeat that. Minimum viable platform, needed **live by 30 September**:

- A person, **team (3–11, self-named)**, centre, school, college or company can claim a minutes target
- A public page per entity showing progress, contributors, and rank
- Minutes captured both in-app and via a **group-sit logging flow** (one host logs N participants × 7 min) — otherwise every off-app sit is invisible
- City-wide live total, visible everywhere
- Volunteer-side view: my recruits, my events, my contribution

If the full build can't land by 30 Sept, ship the group-sit logging and the target-claim page first and let leaderboards follow. Attribution is what makes volunteers work; polish is not.

### Also in P0
- Instrument the **baseline**: lock down Bangalore's current monthly minutes and MAU now, so December's incremental is measurable and not arguable in January
- Pull Youth & Truth performance data (celebrity vs. student content) to settle the events format debate
- Open the Google Health conversation — note their world-record event is **Monday 21 December**, so Bangalore's peak should land before it, not on it

**Exit gate:** note approved by Sadhguru · cause named · platform spec frozen · 1,000+ consecration intents captured.

## PHASE 1 · CORE — 18 August to 30 September

**Goal:** build the spine. 54 anchors, 540 captains, three proven playbooks.

### Volunteer track
1. **Regional kickoff, ~20 August.** All 18 centre coordinators in one room. Each centre picks its own minutes target with guidance — this is the mechanic that made the Bhikshu centre-wise meet work, and it must repeat. Each centre nominates 3 anchors on the spot.
2. **Anchor training, late August.** 54 anchors. Their job for six weeks is one thing only: find and hold 10 captains each.
3. **Captain recruitment, September.** Source from the consecration intents, current Ishangas, Wave-era volunteers, the ~40K active meditators across apps, and IE class closings (Pranathi's experiment — an appeal at a single IE closing yielded 65–70 inquiries; run that at *every* closing).
4. **Captain onboarding is a product moment, not a meeting.** Each captain claims a named target on the platform the day they sign up. If they leave the room without a page of their own, you have a name on a list, not a volunteer.

### Pilot three playbooks in three centres (September)
Do not scale untested procedures into 150 schools. Run and document:
- **School playbook** — the week-long challenge with mandatory daily follow-up, 2 volunteers per school, certificate for every participant (not just winners — the point Smrithi made about not damaging kids' mental health by making it a competition stands)
- **Community sit playbook** — apartment / street / park, one host, 10+ people, logged on the platform
- **Corporate playbook** — one company, one champion, four sits across December

Each playbook = a one-page SOP + a WhatsApp-forwardable kit. If a captain can't run it from the kit alone, it isn't finished.

### Parallel tracks
- **Cause & communication:** name locked, creative developed, the Bangalore mental-health study commissioned if that route is chosen
- **Influencers:** RCB (Smriti Mandhana / Shreyanka Patil / Rajat Patidar), Yash or Rishab Shetty, Rashmika Mandanna, Ayushmann Khurrana — get *soft yeses and dates* in this window, not in November
- **Events:** venues and dates locked; college shortlist confirmed
- **Product:** build

**Exit gate:** 500+ captains with claimed targets · platform live · 3 playbooks documented and proven · celebrity dates soft-confirmed.

**If this gate slips, the December number must be revised down. Do not carry a miss forward.**

## PHASE 2 · MULTIPLY — 1 October to 15 November

**Goal:** 540 → ~9,700. First public visibility.

### The recruitment engine
Each captain holds ~18 volunteers. That is a personal ask to people they already know — colleagues, apartment neighbours, family, their centre's meditator list. Weekly captain check-ins by anchors; the platform shows each captain their recruit count. **Gamify the recruitment layer itself, not just the minutes layer** — this was the two-layer point from the meeting and it belongs here.

Running target: **2,500 by 15 Oct · 5,500 by 31 Oct · 8,000 by 15 Nov.** Track weekly. A miss two weeks running means the cascade is broken somewhere specific — find the layer, don't add central effort on top.

### Public seeding — 10 October (Saturday), World Mental Health Day
Seed the *problem*, not the campaign. Release the Bangalore study or statistics, get coverage, establish why this city and why now. **Do not announce the number here.** A seven-week gap between announcement and Day 1 will go flat, and the agency input in the meeting said exactly this.

### Institutional pipeline (built in this window, executed in December)
- Schools: 15 → **150 signed MOUs**, 2 volunteers assigned to each
- Colleges: 40 confirmed, tied to their own youth festivals where timing allows
- Corporates: 150 companies via the CXO group Sadhguru already met, plus founder networks
- Brand activations scoped (boAt-style booths at SSB and high-footfall points)

**Exit gate:** 8,000+ volunteers each holding a named target · 150 schools and 150 corporates signed · celebrity dates hard-confirmed.

## PHASE 3 · IGNITE — 16 to 30 November

**Goal:** the announcement, and 200K+ pledges banked before Day 1.

- **Sadhguru's five events run in this window** (roughly 10–12 days), mixing college and open-city venues, with direct student interaction as the main format and celebrity conversations as the offbeat layer
- **The announcement lands ~25–28 November.** Sadhguru makes the ask himself. If the events happen without him voicing the call-to-action, the whole chain is carried by second-hand messaging — this was flagged in the meeting and never resolved. Resolve it.
- **Celebrity nomination chain starts immediately after** — pledge seven minutes, nominate the next person. Sequenced so a new name lands every day through to 1 December
- **Pre-registration opens.** Individuals and teams claim December targets *before* the month starts, so Day 1 opens with a number already on the board rather than a zero
- **Volunteer readiness:** every volunteer has their December dates in the calendar and their kit in hand by 30 November

**Exit gate:** 200K+ pre-registered pledges · every volunteer has a dated commitment · Sadhguru's CTA on record.

## PHASE 4 · THE MONTH — 1 to 31 December

**Goal:** 10 million minutes.

**Rhythm.** Daily city total published. Weekly leaderboard resets so late entrants can still win something. Three peak moments to break plateaus:

| Date | Moment |
|---|---|
| **Tue 1 Dec** | Launch — mass simultaneous city sit |
| **Sun 13 Dec** | Mid-month peak — the school and college week culminates |
| **Sun 20 Dec** | Final push — deliberately **before** Google Health's 21 Dec world-record event, so Bangalore's number is set when that lands |
| **Thu 31 Dec** | Close — everyone on the app for the final seven minutes, tied into the New Year gathering (~2 lakh footfall) |

**Weekly operating cadence.** Anchors report centre numbers every Monday. Any centre below 60% of pace gets support that week, not a reminder. The whale skew is expected and fine — but publish a **breadth metric alongside minutes** (unique participants), because that is what makes this a movement rather than 500 devoted people meditating a lot.

**Contingency.** If the 15 December run-rate implies a miss of more than 20%, do not quietly restate the target. Add a fourth peak weekend and lean the corporate and school levers harder — both have the most untapped headroom at that point.

## PHASE 5 · LAND — 1 to 15 January 2027

**Goal:** bank the win, convert to Sankranti, keep the 10,000.

- Declare the result publicly in the first week of January, with the incremental number stated honestly alongside the headline
- Every December participant gets a Makar Sankranti invitation — this was the original purpose of the whole exercise and the point where campaigns usually forget to close the loop
- **Retention is the real prize.** 10,000 activated volunteers is a more durable asset than 10 million minutes. Give every one of them a defined next role before 15 January, or the structure dissolves and the next campaign starts from 60 again
- Written post-mortem with actual conversion rates at each cascade layer — so the next city doesn't rebuild this from scratch

## 3. Critical path and what breaks it

The dependency chain that determines everything:

> **Consecration harvest (by 17 Aug)** → captains recruited (Sept) → **platform live (30 Sept)** → volunteers multiplied (Oct–mid Nov) → institutions signed (by 15 Nov) → announcement (late Nov) → December.

Three failure modes, in order of likelihood:

1. **Platform slips past 30 September.** Then Phase 2 recruits volunteers with nothing to claim and nothing to track, and they go cold before December. *This is the Bhikshu failure, repeating.* Mitigation: ship group-sit logging and target pages first; leaderboards can arrive in November.
2. **The consecration window is missed.** Then Phase 1 starts cold and the captain layer runs 4–6 weeks late, which compresses Phase 2 to nothing. Mitigation: start capture this week, without waiting for approval.
3. **Cause and name drift past mid-August.** Everything downstream — creative, school kits, celebrity pitches, corporate decks — is blocked on it. Mitigation: timebox it. A good-enough name on 10 August beats a perfect one on 10 September.
